Reliable and Interpretable LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluation for Clinical Documentation

In Preparation

Zephylin Dusengimana, et al. · First author

Applied research from work at AfiaSync · targeting a peer-reviewed venue

LLM judges can score the same clinical note differently across runs, and a single aggregate score rarely explains what specifically went wrong — a serious problem in a setting where a single error can affect patient safety. This work studies how design choices, including decomposing a score into separate rubric dimensions (accuracy, completeness, safety), constraining the evaluator's output to a fixed schema, and lowering sampling temperature, affect whether an automated judge's decisions are reliable and interpretable enough for clinicians to act on. The evaluator explicitly lists hallucinated findings and omitted real findings, and is validated against structured clinical notes generated from real encounters.

Yin-AI: A Multimodal Conversational Agent with Voice, Memory, and Document Generation

In Preparation

Collaborative work · Zephylin Dusengimana (co-author)

Multimodal conversational AI · targeting a peer-reviewed venue

Yin-AI is a multimodal conversational agent that unifies voice input, file-context reasoning, persistent cross-session memory, and structured document generation. My contribution centers on evaluation: designing and analyzing how the agent is scored, focusing on response quality measured against human ratings and voice-recognition accuracy across different speakers and noise conditions.
